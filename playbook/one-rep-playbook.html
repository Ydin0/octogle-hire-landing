OctogleHireWHY COLD CALLING

Why cold calling

Every other channel is crowded.
The phone isn't.

Your buyers get pitched all day on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and by cold email. Those channels are crowded because they're easy: AI can run them without a person. A phone call still needs a real person, so far fewer companies do it well.

Call a valid number and the person picks up 25–35% of the time. That's a live conversation with exactly the customer you want, every time. Here's what one rep's month looks like:

Ask yourself: which other channel puts you in a live conversation with 600 of your ideal customers a month? Give the rep a good pitch with something useful to offer, like a free audit or a guide, and those conversations turn into meetings.
