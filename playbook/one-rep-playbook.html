What to look for in a rep

1

They've done outbound before. Ask how many dials a day they made and how many meetings came out of it. Anyone who can't answer in numbers hasn't run a phone day.

2

Role-play the opener before you hire. Five minutes on a mock call tells you more than an hour of interview.

3

Their hours are your buyers' hours. A rep who calls when your market is at its desk books more than a better rep who calls at the wrong time.

OctogleHirePARTS 2 AND 3 · THE LIST AND THE DIAL

2Part 2

The list: mobiles,
not switchboards.

4

Call the person who signs, on their mobile. A switchboard gets you a gatekeeper and "they're in a meeting". Build the list before the day starts, so the rep spends the morning dialling, not in a spreadsheet.

5

One market per list. Same industry, same size, same problem. The rep gets sharper on every call because every call sounds the same.

6

Never call the same person twice by accident. Remove anyone already in your CRM before the list goes live.{{TPS}}

3Part 3

The dial: the next call rings
before the last one's down.

7

Use a power dialer. When a call ends, the rep taps the outcome and the next lead rings. Dialling by hand wastes most of the hour between calls.

8

Call from local numbers. People answer numbers that look local. Match the caller ID to the market you're calling.

9

Block the hours that connect. Watch which hours people pick up, and put the calling blocks there, not where they suit the diary.

OctogleHirePART 4 · THE CALL

4Part 4

Book it on the call.
Not "I'll send some times".

The job of a cold call is the meeting, not the sale. Get to the point in one breath, then agree a slot while they're still on the line.

The opener

"Hi [name], it's [rep] from [company]. I'll be quick. We work with [type of business] on [the outcome they want]. Is [the problem you solve] something you're dealing with at the moment?"

If yes

"Makes sense. Easiest thing is 15 minutes with [closer] to show you how it works. I've got [day] at [time] or [day] at [time]. Which suits?"

10

Offer two times, not an open question. "When suits you?" gets "send me something". Two slots gets a choice.

11

Book it while they're on the line. Open the booking page, put them in, and read the time back before you hang up.

12

Ask one qualifying question before you book. Booking people who were never a fit fills the calendar and empties the sit rate.

OctogleHirePART 5 · THE SIT RATE

5Part 5

A booked meeting is worth nothing
until they turn up.

Between the booking and the call, the prospect should hear from you enough that turning up feels like the obvious thing to do.

13

Confirm in the same minute. Invite plus a short confirmation by email, text and WhatsApp while they still remember saying yes.

14

Send a 20-second voice note from the rep. "Looking forward to Thursday, here's what we'll cover." A real person is much harder to stand up.

15

Remind them twice. The day before and 30 minutes before, with the link.

16

Chase a no-show the same day. A call and a message to rebook. A no-show is usually busy, not lost.

Every new lead gets a call within a minute

Inbound leads go cold in minutes. Call while they're still on your website, and if you miss them, follow up on a schedule until they reply:
